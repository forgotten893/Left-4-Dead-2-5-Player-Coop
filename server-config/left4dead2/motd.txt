Welcome! Type these in chat (with ! or /) or in the console (with sm_ in front).

CHARACTERS  (Survivor Chat Select 2)
  !csm  or  !model    Open the menu to pick your survivor (all 8 on L4D2 campaigns).
                      Your pick is saved and comes back when you rejoin.
  !voice              Change just your survivor's voice.

CHAPTER STATS  (Last Chapter Results)
  !lastresults  or  !slr   Every survivor's stats from the previous chapter.
  !results  or  !sr        Every survivor's stats for this chapter so far.
                           Shows the end-of-chapter highlights (Tank Slayer, Witch Hunter, Team Medic...)
                           for all survivors, a stats line per survivor in chat, and a full table in your console.

VOICE LINES  (Vocalize Spam)
  Spam the vocalize menu like in L4D1 - new lines play on top of the old ones.
  sm_vlines laugh     (console) List your survivor's lines containing a word.
  sm_vline laughter12 (console) Say one exact line.
  Key binds work too, e.g.  bind v "vocalize PlayerLaugh"   or   bind b "sm_vline laughter12"

GENERAL  (SourceMod)
  !revote             Change your answer while a vote is running.
  motd                (type in chat, no !) Show this message again.
